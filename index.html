About this site

Three categories of documented harm

The published record is organised into three categories. The supporting pages provide the detailed chronology, names, and source basis.

Category 1To lenders and investors

Mr. Tothfalussy has failed to satisfy multiple commercial loans across jurisdictions. These include an internationally enforceable HKIAC arbitration award of approximately USD 300,000 (October 2023, Case HKIAC/23011), an unpaid Hermelindis Stiftung claim of approximately USD 800,000 plus missed interest of $16,000 per month since July 2024, and a group of UK investors whose claim has resulted in a final judgment of $1,248,420.28 plus applicable interest against Secondlife Electronics Trading LLC in Miami-Dade County. Further documented investor claims account for approximately USD 500,000. Aggregate documented unpaid exposure is around USD 3 million.

Category 2To consumers

The Trustpilot page for Tothfalussy's (meanwhile suspended) palnecashop.de carries 12 one-star reviews and an aggregate score of 1.9, the lowest the platform's algorithm produces. Customer accounts dating from March 2024 onward describe counterfeit Apple products sold as genuine, money taken without delivery of goods, ignored emails, and unreachable phone numbers. By Mr. Tothfalussy's own sworn statement filed in court in April 2026, the Amazon seller account for the same operation has been terminated by Amazon.

Category 3To accountability mechanisms

Mr. Tothfalussy has not participated in the HKIAC arbitration. He has used and keeps using family addresses and does not provide servable addresses to creditors. He has changed corporate vehicles repeatedly. In the Miami-Dade litigation he advanced a defense theory that attributed the consequences of his own conduct to an unrelated third party. When he cannot or will not meet an obligation, blame migrates outward. That theory did not prevent the Court from entering final judgment against Tothfalussy's company.
