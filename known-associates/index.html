Ivan Kolompar filings

Reutilise Solutions Ltd · identity discrepancy requiring verification

UK Companies House contains a person-with-significant-control filing under the name Ivan Kolompar, notified with effect from 8 August 2023, and a director appointment under the same name from 18 October 2024. On that same October date, Andras Tothfalussy’s filed roles as director, secretary, and person with significant control ended.

The two Ivan Kolompar entries contain different birth years: July 1982 in the control filing and July 1984 in the director filing. The public register therefore does not establish whether these entries concern one person with inconsistent filed data or two people with the same name.

Reutilise Solutions Ltd was compulsorily struck off and dissolved on 20 May 2025. A Palneca GmbH shareholder list entered in the German register on 10 November 2025 nevertheless names Reutilise Solutions Ltd as Palneca’s sole shareholder.

The sequence is a corporate-control transition that warrants verification. The filings do not establish nominee status, explain the commercial terms of any transfer, resolve the identity discrepancy, or establish wrongdoing by either role-holder.

Sources: UK Companies House, Reutilise Solutions Ltd, 14000243; German commercial register, Palneca GmbH, HRB 251621.
